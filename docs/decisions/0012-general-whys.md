# 0012 — Every rule carries a general why, never an anecdote

[README](../../README.md) · [Decisions](index.md)

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-01)

## Decision
Each rule in the process states the general reason it exists. The incident
that produced it stays in the project's protocol log.

## Why
An agent matches an anecdote by its surface shape and misses the same
problem in a different shape. A general reason applies to cases nobody has
seen yet — and keeps one project's details out of a public plugin.
